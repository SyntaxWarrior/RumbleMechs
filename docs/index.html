RumbleMechs

A 2D side-scrolling multiplayer team PvP mech game with destructible terrain.
Blast through hills, dig under the enemy's defenses, and build your own.

Features

  • Multiplayer

    Play with up to 16 people in team-based Capture the Flag.

  • Fully destructible terrain

    Dig tunnels and surprise your enemy. Create a secret tunnel right into the enemy base.

  • Physics

    Mechs, crates, grenades and rounds all move by the same physics. Blow a hole in a wall and lob grenades through it.

  • Fog of war

    Vision is shared by the team. If the enemy can't see you, they don't know what you are doing.

  • Build structures

    Build turrets, shield generators and more to defend your base and break through the enemy's.

  • Your own loadout

    Build your mech to suit your playstyle: heavy or light, machine guns or grenade launchers. But you only have so much space.

Your own loadout

Combine your loadout and find the kit that works for you. Choose a class (Hawk, Raptor or Marauder) and plating, then fit weapons and modules on the mech within your weight capacity. Save up to five presets.

Download and play

You download a small launcher with the game inside. Every time you start it, the launcher checks for a new version, installs it and then starts the game, so you only have to download once.

Windows (x64) Linux (x64)

Windows

  1. Unzip it anywhere.
  2. Start RumbleMechsLauncher.exe.
  3. Pick a server from the list and play.

Windows may warn that the program is from an unknown publisher, since it isn't signed. Choose More info and then Run anyway.

Linux

64-bit, with a desktop and OpenGL.

tar -xzf RumbleMechs-linux-x64.tar.gz
cd RumbleMechs
./RumbleMechsLauncher

If it won't start, make it runnable first: chmod +x RumbleMechsLauncher game/RumbleMechs

Nothing else needs installing. Type the name you want to play under in the box above the server list. All builds are on the releases page.
